Hack4Impact student committee

Web and mobile apps for nonprofit organizations

Hack4Impact Polytechnique Montréal brings together Polytechnique students who build web and mobile apps for nonprofit organizations.

Students and nonprofits interested in working with the committee can get in touch.

Nonprofits

For nonprofits

A nonprofit that needs a website, an app or a tool to manage its data can hand the project to a team of students.

Once finished, the tool belongs to the organization, which receives the code and the documentation.

Propose a project
  1. 01

    First call

    A first meeting to understand the organization and its needs.

  2. 02

    Project plan

    The feature list is written with the organization, then ranked by priority.

  3. 03

    Design and development

    The team designs the mockups then builds the tool, presenting its progress to the organization along the way to gather feedback.

  4. 04

    Delivery

    The tool goes online and the organization's staff learns how to use it.

Students

For students

The committee recruits Polytechnique students from every program for developer, designer and project manager roles.

Members work in small teams on a real project with a real organization.

Discover the roles Join the team
Is experience required?
Not necessarily. Tech lead roles require a few programming courses. For the other roles, motivation matters most.
How much time does it take?
Between 5 and 10 hours a week, including team meetings, development and meetings with the organization.
When does recruitment open?
Interested students can contact the committee to be notified when recruitment opens.
