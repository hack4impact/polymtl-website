Canopée
Organization
Réseau Canopée
Year
2026
Platforms
Web, iOS and Android

Web and mobile app for the volunteer patrollers of the Réseau Canopée, who monitor the wooded areas of Laval. Volunteers record their patrols with GPS and report what they see in the field with a photo and location. The Canopée team follows everything on a map.
