Roles

Every project team brings together developers, designers and product managers. Here is what each role does and delivers over the course of a project.

Engineering

Developer

The developer builds the tool on a team of product managers, tech leads, designers and 3 to 5 other developers.

Responsibilities
  • Build a project that helps a nonprofit accelerate its daily workflow.
  • Learn a modern tech stack: React, Node.js, Express, TypeScript, PostgreSQL and more.
  • Work as a team on real tasks, similar to working in industry.
  • Take part in events with the Hack4Impact community.
  • Help nonprofits achieve their mission.

Design

Product Designer

The designer understands the purpose of the product and makes it usable and accessible to a diverse group of people.

Responsibilities
Understand the purpose of the product, identify possible pain points and find ways to make the product more usable and accessible, with user testing when needed. Make decisions about important product features and visual design elements, and check with the product managers and tech leads that the designs are feasible and practical.
Deliverables
  • User flow and information architecture
  • User journey
  • Wireframes and visual design
  • Interview notes and documentation
  • Style guide, if needed
  • Case study and design thinking document
  • Heuristic analysis

Product

Product Manager

The product manager is the main point of contact for the nonprofit and owns the overall direction of the project.

Responsibilities
Communicate with the nonprofit weekly by email and meet every two weeks for feedback. Work with designers to make sure the product meets user needs, and with the tech leads for a technical perspective. Keep project documentation, lead sprint planning, own the roadmap and build an inclusive and exciting team culture.
Deliverables
  • Feature spec, roadmap and user journey
  • Product proposal
  • Final presentation
  • Client and team meeting notes
